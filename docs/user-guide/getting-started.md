# Getting Started with NativeCRM

Welcome to NativeCRM! We built this tool to help you spend less time on data entry and more time building relationships with your customers. Whether you are a solo founder tracking your first leads or part of a growing sales team, this guide will walk you through everything you need to get up and running.

---

## What Is NativeCRM and Why Should I Care?

NativeCRM is a customer relationship management tool that uses artificial intelligence to do the busywork for you. It automatically tracks your emails and meetings, summarizes conversations, scores your deals so you know where to focus, and even drafts follow-up emails on your behalf. Instead of spending your morning updating spreadsheets, you open NativeCRM and everything is already there, organized and ready for action.

---

## Signing Up and Logging In

Getting into NativeCRM takes about 30 seconds.

1. Go to **app.nativecrm.com** in your web browser.
2. Click **Sign Up**.
3. Choose how you want to log in:
   - **Sign in with Google** -- use your Gmail or Google Workspace account.
   - **Sign in with Microsoft** -- use your Outlook or Microsoft 365 account.
4. A popup will ask you to grant NativeCRM permission to verify your identity. Click **Allow**.
5. You will be asked to name your **workspace**. This is your team's shared space. Pick something simple like your company name (e.g., "Acme Sales").
6. That's it. You are in.

[Screenshot: The sign-up screen showing the Google and Microsoft login buttons]

> **Already have a workspace?** If a colleague invited you, check your email for the invitation link. Click it and sign in -- you will be added to the existing workspace automatically.

## Your First Look: The Dashboard

After signing in, you land on the **Dashboard**. Think of it as your morning newspaper for sales.

[Screenshot: The main dashboard showing pipeline overview, deal metrics, and recent activity feed]

Here is what you are looking at:

- **Pipeline Overview** -- A summary of all your deals, grouped by stage (like "Qualification," "Proposal," "Negotiation"). You can see at a glance how much revenue is in play.
- **Deal Metrics** -- Key numbers like total pipeline value, deals closing this month, and win rate.
- **Recent Activities** -- A live feed showing the latest things that happened: emails received, meetings held, deals that moved forward.

Don't worry about understanding everything right now. The dashboard gets more useful as you add data.

## Adding Your First Contact

A contact is any person you do business with -- a lead, a customer, a partner. Let's add one.

1. Click **Contacts** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **+ New Contact** button in the top-right corner.
3. Fill in the basics:
   - **Name** -- Their full name (e.g., "Jane Smith").
   - **Email** -- Their email address.
   - **Company** -- The company they work for. Start typing and NativeCRM will suggest matches, or you can create a new company on the spot.
   - **Phone** -- Optional, but handy.
4. Click **Save**.

[Screenshot: The new contact form with fields filled in for a sample contact]

That's your first contact. You will see their profile page, which will fill up with activity as you interact with them.

> **Pro tip:** You do not have to add every contact by hand. Once you connect your email (next section), NativeCRM will automatically create contacts from the people you correspond with.

## Creating Your First Deal

A deal represents a potential sale -- something you are working toward closing. Let's create one.

1. Click **Deals** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Deal**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Deal Name** -- A short description (e.g., "Acme Corp -- Annual License").
   - **Value** -- How much the deal is worth (e.g., "$12,000").
   - **Contact** -- The person you are working with on this deal.
   - **Stage** -- Where the deal is right now. If you just started talking, choose **Qualification**. The stages are:
     - **Qualification** -- You are figuring out if this is a real opportunity.
     - **Discovery** -- You are learning about their needs.
     - **Proposal** -- You have sent or are preparing a proposal.
     - **Negotiation** -- You are working out terms.
     - **Closed Won** -- You got the deal!
     - **Closed Lost** -- It didn't work out this time.
   - **Expected Close Date** -- Your best guess of when this will close.
4. Click **Save**.

[Screenshot: The new deal form with sample data filled in]

Your deal now appears on the **Pipeline Board** -- a visual board where you can drag deals between stages as they progress.

## Connecting Your Email

This is where NativeCRM starts to feel like magic. When you connect your email, the system automatically:

- Pulls in your last 30 days of email history.
- Creates contacts from the people you have been emailing.
- Links emails to the right contacts, companies, and deals.
- Keeps syncing new emails as they arrive.

Here is how to set it up:

1. Click the **Settings** icon (gear) in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar.
2. Click **Integrations**.
3. Under **Email**, click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook** (whichever you use).
4. A popup will ask for permission to read your email. This is safe -- NativeCRM only reads business correspondence and never sends email on your behalf without your explicit approval.
5. Click **Allow**.
6. The initial sync begins. You will see a progress indicator. For most accounts, this takes a few minutes.

[Screenshot: The integrations settings page showing the email connection options]

> **Privacy note:** NativeCRM only syncs email metadata and content for CRM purposes. You can disconnect at any time, and your email data will be removed from NativeCRM.

## What Happens Next: Auto-Sync Magic

Once your email is connected, NativeCRM works quietly in the background:

- **New emails** appear on the activity timeline of the relevant contact, company, or deal -- usually within a few minutes.
- **New contacts** are automatically created when you email someone for the first time (or they email you). You will see them in your Contacts list marked as auto-created.
- **AI meeting summaries** are generated after any calendar meeting that has been synced. You will find summaries on the **AI Insights** tab of the related contact or deal.
- **Deal health scores** update continuously based on your email and meeting activity. A number from 0 to 100 tells you how healthy each deal is.

You don't have to do anything. Just work normally, and NativeCRM keeps your CRM up to date.

## Quick Wins: 5 Things to Do in Your First 10 Minutes

Now that you are set up, here are five things to do right now to get the most out of NativeCRM:

### 1. Connect Your Calendar
Go to **Settings > Integrations** and connect Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar. This links your meetings to contacts and enables AI meeting summaries.

### 2. Import Your Existing Contacts
Have a spreadsheet of contacts? Go to **Contacts > Import** and upload a CSV file. NativeCRM will match columns automatically.

### 3. Create 3-5 Active Deals
Add the deals you are currently working on. Even rough estimates are fine. This populates your pipeline board so you can start tracking progress visually.

### 4. Try the Command Palette
Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows) to open the global search. Type a contact name, company, or deal. This is the fastest way to find anything in NativeCRM.

### 5. Explore a Contact's Timeline
Click on any contact and look at their **Activity Timeline**. You will see every email, meeting, call, and note in chronological order. This is your single source of truth for any relationship.
