Everything you need to know about NativeCRM.

NativeCRM connects to your email (Gmail or Outlook) and calendar via OAuth. It automatically creates contacts from email correspondents, logs email threads as activities, and generates deal records from meeting patterns. AI enrichment then fills in company details, job titles, and other fields from public sources.
