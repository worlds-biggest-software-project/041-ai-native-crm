# NativeCRM Product Overview

## Your CRM Should Work for You, Not the Other Way Around

**NativeCRM is the open-source, AI-native CRM that eliminates manual data entry and gives your sales team back 5+ hours every week.**

Unlike legacy CRMs that bolt on AI as an afterthought, NativeCRM was built from the ground up with intelligence at its core. Every contact, every deal update, every follow-up draft --- generated automatically from the conversations your team is already having.

---

## Key Features

**Intelligent Pipeline Population**
Sync Gmail and Outlook to automatically capture contacts, deals, and activities --- no manual entry, no missed opportunities.

**ML-Driven Deal Scoring**
Every deal gets a real-time health score from 0--100, powered by machine learning that analyzes engagement patterns, email sentiment, and historical win rates.

**AI Meeting Summaries & Follow-Ups**
LLM-powered summaries distill hour-long calls into key takeaways, action items, and draft follow-up emails --- ready to send in seconds.

**GDPR-Safe Contact Enrichment**
Automatically enrich contact profiles with firmographic and professional data while maintaining full GDPR and privacy compliance.

**Visual Kanban Pipeline**
Drag-and-drop pipeline management with customizable stages, activity timelines, and global search (Cmd+K) for instant access to any record.

**Developer-First Extensibility**
REST API, webhooks with HMAC signing, custom fields/objects, workflow automation, MCP server integration, and CSV/JSON export. Build anything on top.
